考照流程How it works

18 岁以上在康州考驾照的步骤Getting a Connecticut license at 18+

  1. 考取学习许可Get a learner's permit

    在 DMV 通过视力测试和 25 题笔试。Pass the vision test and 25-question knowledge test at the DMV.

  2. 本校提供We offer

    上 8 小时安全课Take the 8-hour course

    任何时间都可以上，路考前完成即可。Any time, as long as it is done before the road test.

  3. 持许可满 90 天Hold the permit 90 days

    持学习许可至少 90 天后才能约路考。You must hold the permit at least 90 days before booking the road test.

  4. 本校提供We offer

    练车Practice driving

    跟教练上道路驾驶课。Lessons with our instructor.

  5. 本校提供We offer

    参加路考Take the road test

    用驾校的车，教练陪同。In our school car, with an instructor.
